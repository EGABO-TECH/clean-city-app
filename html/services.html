Recycling & Special Programs

Going beyond collection - we turn waste into value and protect our environment.

Plastic Recycling Initiative

We collect, sort, and process plastic waste into reusable pellets and products. Our program supports local recycling entrepreneurs and reduces plastic pollution in Uganda's cities and waterways.

E-Waste Management

Safe disposal of electronic waste including computers, phones, and appliances. We ensure data destruction, component recovery, and environmentally sound disposal of hazardous materials.

Composting Program

Organic waste from markets and households is converted into high-quality compost for agriculture. This reduces landfill use and supports sustainable farming across Uganda.

School Education Program

We partner with schools to teach students about waste management, recycling, and environmental stewardship. Free workshops and materials for participating schools.

How Our Services Work

A simple, transparent process from start to finish.

Step 1: Report or Schedule

Use our website, mobile app, or call your district station. Provide location, waste type, and volume.

Step 2: Get Your ETA

Our system calculates the estimated pickup time and assigns the nearest available collection team.

Step 3: Confirmation Call

You receive a confirmation call or SMS with the exact pickup window and team details.

Step 4: Collection & Documentation

Our team arrives, collects the waste, and provides you with a collection receipt or digital confirmation.

Step 5: Recycling & Disposal

Waste is transported to our sorting facility, recycled where possible, and disposed of responsibly.
