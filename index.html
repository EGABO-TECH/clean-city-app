How It Works

Four simple steps to a cleaner Uganda. From reporting to collection, we handle it all.

1. Report Waste

Use our online form or call your district station. Tell us the location, type, and approximate volume of waste.

2. Get ETA

We instantly calculate an estimated pickup time based on your location, volume, and available collection trucks.

3. We Collect

Our trained team arrives at the scheduled time, collects the waste safely, and transports it to the right facility.

4. Recycle & Dispose

Waste is sorted, recycled where possible, and disposed of responsibly to protect our environment.
